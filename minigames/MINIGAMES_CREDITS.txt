Custom community emoji
The custom Discord emoji originate from the user's supplied shop catalogue and
community Discord CDN images. Their creators retain their rights; these images
are not relicensed as Twemoji artwork. Some unbundled/new custom emoji are fetched
from Discord's CDN at runtime. Names and text badges still appear in embeds if
an external image is unavailable; the picture falls back to name initials.
