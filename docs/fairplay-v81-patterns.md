# v81 personal opening move patterns

Page7 now groups a legal first-ten-ply prefix by the subject's first five SAN
moves and the opponent's first move, separately for White/Black. A recognized
opening family from that prefix is secondary context. Later moves do not alter
the label. The family name may be shortened for the legend; the five own moves
remain intact. Short games are explicitly marked; illegal sequences are Unknown.

These are move habits, not identical-position clusters: the opponent's later
moves may differ. They never feed scoring or imply engine assistance. Top-ten
selection, frequency floors, Other, period shading and rating plots remain.
